Vacation & Second-Home Cleanouts — common questions

Can you do the job if I'm not in Vermont?

Yes, most of our condo and second-home jobs are done for owners who aren't on-site. We coordinate access and send photos when we're finished.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. We're used to working within HOA rules around Stratton Mountain, Bromley and Okemo — loading zones, elevator reservations and quiet hours.

Which towns do you offer vacation & second-home cleanouts in?

We provide vacation & second-home cleanouts throughout Southern Vermont, including Manchester, Stratton, Bondville/Winhall, Dorset, Londonderry, Peru, Jamaica, Arlington, Ludlow, Chester, Grafton, Bennington, Brattleboro and Killington.

Is Vermont Junk insured?

Yes. Vermont Junk LLC carries business insurance including workers' comp and is a registered carrier (USDOT #7245571). A certificate of insurance is available on request for HOAs, property managers and commercial clients.
